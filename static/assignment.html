National Railway Critical Tracks

Discover single points of failure across a 16-station passenger rail network connecting 4 regional commuter hubs.

Scenario & Problem Statement

The National Transit Authority manages 16 passenger railway stations (0 to 15) connected by 20 bidirectional railway tracks across 4 regional commuter hubs: Central Metropolis, Maritime Lake District, Mountain Pass Basin, and Alpine Glacier Terminus. Identify all critical tracks (bridges) whose failure would split the national rail system into disconnected clusters!
